Act VI Your first visit

What happens when you reach out.

No pitch deck. No pressure. A consultation built like a records appointment — so the first conversation is already useful.

  1. Step 01 · About 3 minutes

    Records

    Tell us about your practice in four short steps. Before we speak, a strategist reviews your market, your search presence and your reviews.

  2. Step 02 · 30 minutes

    Consultation

    A video or phone call with a strategist — not a salesperson. We walk you through what we found, including what you’re already doing well.

  3. Step 03 · Yours to keep

    Treatment plan

    A written plan with priorities, milestones and a clear investment. It’s yours to keep, whether or not we work together.

Before you book

Do you only work with orthodontists?

Orthodontic practices are our specialism — it’s in our name. Our strategy, our creative and our podcast are built around how patients and parents actually choose an orthodontist.

How soon will we see results?

Paid search can generate consultation requests within weeks. Organic search, reviews and reputation compound over months. Your treatment plan sets milestones for both, and monthly force reports show progress against them.

Do we need a new website?

Not always. Records tell us whether your current site can be aligned or should be rebuilt. If it’s working, we’ll tell you.

We already have an agency. Can we still book?

Yes. Many consultations are second opinions. You’ll leave with an objective read on what’s working, whatever you decide.

What does it cost?

Investment depends on your market, your goals and the phases you need. Your treatment plan states it clearly — no surprises.
